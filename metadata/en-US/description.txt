Tack is a focused, adaptive task app for iPhone and iPad. It pairs a sharp everyday workflow with native Notion and Obsidian sync — so your tasks live where you actually think.

CAPTURE FAST
Type naturally. Tack parses your words: "Buy milk tomorrow 5pm #groceries" becomes a structured task — due date, tags, priority inferred in milliseconds. Add a task from the lock screen, Siri, the share sheet, or the in-app quick add.

TODAY, INBOX, LISTS, STATS
Five views, no friction. Today surfaces what is actually due. Inbox holds raw captures until you sort them. Lists are your own grouping. Stats gamifies the practice with a streak counter and a fourteen-day completion chart.

WIDGETS
Today, Inbox and Stats widgets — small, medium and large. Tap to complete. Long-press to jump into the matching view. They refresh on the hour and reload on every change.

SIRI, SHORTCUTS, SHARE SHEET
"Add to Tack: review the design doc"
"Mark done in Tack: take out the trash"
A Quick Capture intent for the Share Sheet. Three intents ship with the app; more on the roadmap.

NOTION, OBSIDIAN, NOTHING ELSE
A single, opinionated sync engine — talk to Notion when your tasks live in a database, or to your Obsidian vault when they live in markdown. Bidirectional, fault-tolerant, YAML front-matter on every file. No lock-in: your tasks are valid markdown and valid Notion rows before Tack touches them.

DESIGN DETAILS
- Dark and light themes with semantic tokens (the `TK` family)
- Six built-in locales: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese
- Adaptive shell: tabbed on iPhone, split on iPad
- 100% offline. Your tasks live in SwiftData; iCloud backs them up automatically

Tack is built with native SwiftUI and SwiftData. There is no server. There are no ads. There is no tracking.
