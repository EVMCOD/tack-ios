v1.0 — Initial release

A native task app for iPhone and iPad with bidirectional Obsidian sync, three interactive Home Screen widgets, and natural-language quick add.

What's inside:
- Today, Inbox, Lists, Stats, Settings
- Quick Add with natural-language parsing (#tag, !priority, tomorrow 5pm, in 3 days)
- Home Screen widgets: Today, Inbox, Stats (small, medium, large)
- AppIntents: Add, Complete, Quick Capture (Siri, Shortcuts, share sheet)
- Obsidian vault folder with YAML frontmatter round-trip
- Streak counter and 14-day completion chart
- Six locales: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese
